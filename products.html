Styling & Reception Etiquette

Favor Presentation & Placement Guide

From charger plates to midnight sparkler departures, discover four elegant ways to showcase your personalized keepsakes at your celebration.

Place Setting
01

The Individual Charger Setting

Centered gracefully on folded French linen napkins or layered atop gold charger plates. Adorned with a handwritten calligraphy escort card and a delicate botanical sprig.

Stylist Tip: Best suited for monogrammed linen boxes, sweet jars, or mini scented soy candles.
1 Favor Per Guest Plan Setting
Welcome Credenza
02

The Escort & Greeting Table

Arranged in dramatic tiered symmetry on an antique credenza at cocktail entrance. Guests discover their favor alongside their seating card and a flute of vintage champagne.

Stylist Tip: Elevate heights using brass pedestals, floral runners, and floating votive candles.
Cocktail Arrival Plan Setting
Hotel Suite
03

Destination Welcome Suite

Delivered directly to hotel guest rooms upon arrival. Bundles custom keepsakes, organic artisan sweets, luggage tags, and your wedding weekend schedule in a custom tote.

Stylist Tip: Include a personalized handwritten thank-you note from the couple.
1 Per Couple Plan Setting
Grand Finale
04

Midnight Departure Favor Bar

Staged near the grand ballroom exit as the final song plays. Handed to departing guests alongside late-night sweet treats, hot cider cones, or mini celebratory prosecco bottles.

Stylist Tip: Station wedding attendants to hand favors out during the sparkler exit send-off.
Sparkler Send-Off Plan Setting
