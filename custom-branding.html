Couture Craftsmanship

Our Signature Personalization Services

Every favor is individually crafted using time-honored artisanal techniques and high-precision modern equipment.

Hot Foil Stamping

Traditional heat-pressed metallic foils in Royal Champagne Gold, Romantic Rose Gold, Sterling Silver, and Emerald. Indented deep into heavy cotton stock.

Handmade Wax Seals

Custom brass dies pressed into flexible, shatterproof sealing wax. Available in over 18 wedding shades with organic botanical or monogram impressions.

Laser Etching & Engraving

Ultra-fine laser detailing on natural wood coasters, glass candle jars, and stainless steel flasks for permanent, indelible beauty.

Hand-Dyed Silk Ribbons

Raw-edged chiffon and 100% mulberry silk ribbons naturally plant-dyed to match your floral arrangements and bridesmaid palette.

Calligraphy & Letterpress

Old-world Heidelberg letterpress printing paired with bespoke dip-pen calligraphy for name cards, escort tags, and thank-you notes.

Bespoke Rigid Boxes

Custom-sized rigid boxes wrapped in fine textured linen paper with magnetic closures, foam inserts, and debossed emblems.
