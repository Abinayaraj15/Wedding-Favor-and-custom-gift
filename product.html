Couture Favor Packaging & Handcrafted Detail

Our signature luxury wedding favor boxes are precision-formed using rigid 1200gsm archival board wrapped in hand-milled textured Italian linen. Each box is accented with your deeply embossed monogram in brilliant metallic hot foil and tied by hand with generous yards of custom-dyed crinkle silk chiffon ribbon.

Designed as an enduring keepsake, this favor box is sized to hold gourmet confections, macarons, hand-poured travel candles, or delicate bridal jewelry that your wedding guests will treasure forever.

How Our Artisanal Proofing Works

  • Step 1 — Submit Your Details: Enter your couple names, wedding date, monogram, and chosen metallic foil & ribbon finishes.
  • Step 2 — Digital PDF Proof (within 24 Hours): Our design team crafts a 1:1 scale visual proof showing your foil stamping and tag layout.
  • Step 3 — Revisions Welcome: Request adjustments to spacing, wording, or typography until your suite is flawless.
  • Step 4 — Handcrafted Production: Once approved, our atelier casts your custom brass die, hot-stamps your boxes, and hand-ties each ribbon.

The Atelier 5-Point Quality Promise

Every custom keepsake is handcrafted in our studio with archival-grade materials, precision brass dies, and complete peace of mind for your wedding day.

01

1:1 Digital Proof Approval

Within 24 hours of your order, our master typographers draft an exact 1:1 scale vector PDF proof of your monogram, foil alignment, and wax seals. Production only begins once you sign off.

Unlimited Proof Edits
02

FSC Italian Archival Stock

We use 1200gsm rigid archival board sourced from sustainably managed Tuscan paper mills. Acid-free and chlorine-free, ensuring your favor keepsakes never yellow or warp over decades.

100% Eco-Certified
03

Solid Brass Hot-Foil Dies

No flaking digital toner foil. We custom-machine individual solid brass dies, pressing genuine European hot foil into linen paper at 400°F for deeply indented, heirloom-quality brilliance.

Custom Brass Cast
04

Every favor is individually nested in custom slotted trays with corner guards. In the rare event of transit damage, we rush identical replacement keepsakes overnight via priority air at zero cost.

Zero-Breakage Guarantee
